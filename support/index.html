Support

Need help with Pip & Pebble?

Email support at daniel.elliott.lewis@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or Android version, and a short description of what happened.

Common fixes

  • If the widget is not updating, open Pip & Pebble once so the app can refresh the widget data.
  • If the countdown looks wrong, check the reunion date and selected time format in the app.
  • If a widget is blank after installing, remove the widget from the home screen, open the app, and add the widget again.
  • If notifications are missing, confirm notifications are enabled for Pip & Pebble in system settings.
